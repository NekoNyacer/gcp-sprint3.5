# 過渡素材生成記錄

使用內建 imagegen，分別以原 `../jump.png`、`../food.png` 為參考，並要求透明背景。產生的兩張 `*-transition-study.png` 保留原圖，仍是研究圖：圖內相鄰角色的鬍鬚與尾巴可能相碰，不可直接按格線無損切割。跳躍第 2 至 3 格仍有體態突變；進食在第 2 至 4 格仍有突變，後四格近似重複。不可將這些素材描述為已完成平滑過渡。

## Jump

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: animation transition reference sheet, intended for 2D sprite animation. Input 1 is the approved jumping character and action: use exactly the same realistic but cute chipmunk, russet-brown fur, cream belly and black-and-cream back stripes, huge bushy tail, side view facing right. Create exactly 8 individual full-body drawings arranged left-to-right in two rows of four, equal transparent cells, generous gutters; no overlaps, no cropped tails, toes or whiskers. These must be eight consecutive, CLOSELY SPACED in-between poses from image 1's fourth pose (extended upward airborne) to image 1's fifth pose (tucked, compact in midair), not separate unrelated actions. Gradually and anatomically bend knees and elbows, pull all four paws towards chest, transition torso from long stretch to compact tuck, let tail smoothly follow; maintain same head and eyes, animal proportions, lighting and hair detail, fixed camera and consistent scale. Do not introduce resting or ground contact. No floor, props, text, lines or other characters. Genuine transparent background.

## Food

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: animation transition reference sheet, intended for 2D sprite animation. Input 1 is the approved food sequence character and action: use exactly the same realistic but cute russet chipmunk with black-and-cream stripes, cream belly, big eyes and bushy tail. Create exactly 8 individual full-body drawings arranged left-to-right in two rows of four, equal transparent cells, generous gutters; no overlaps, no cropped tails, toes or whiskers. These must be eight consecutive, closely spaced in-between poses linking image 1's third pose (crouched reaching for red berry) to image 1's fourth pose (sitting upright holding same red berry in both paws): lift the berry with forepaws, gradually raise head and torso, transfer weight onto haunches, bring paws and berry towards chest, end sitting upright. Keep its feet on one consistent baseline and the berry visible in every pose; do not add any new actions. Keep same face, body size, fur, pose scale and camera across frames, show gradual changes and believable anatomy. Genuine transparent background, no floor, no text, no other props, no hand, no other animals.
